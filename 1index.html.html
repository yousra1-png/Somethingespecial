A letter from me to you

take your time reading this

My Jelyann,

I don't really know where to begin, because there are some feelings that seem too big to fit neatly into words.

But I wanted to try anyway.

If the universe is really as enormous as we know it is, then sometimes I think it's strange and beautiful that two people can find each other at all.

Out of all the places we could have been, all the people we could have met, all the conversations we could have never had — somehow, ours happened.

And I'm really, really grateful that it did.

You have become part of so many little moments in my days. Sometimes it's a conversation. Sometimes it's a random thought that makes me smile.

Sometimes it's simply knowing that somewhere out there is someone who matters to me.

I made this little universe because I wanted to give you something that felt a little different.

Something you could explore slowly.

Something that could hold more than one sentence.

And most importantly, something that could remind you that distance doesn't erase the beautiful things two people create between them.

There is so much more I could say. So many memories I could write down. So many things I still want to tell you.

But maybe that's the nicest part — that our story isn't supposed to fit inside one little letter.

There are still chapters left.

So many stars left to name.

So many ordinary days that might someday become our favorite memories.

And for now, I'm just happy that somewhere beneath this enormous sky, you are you, and I am me, and somehow we found each other.

Always,
Yousra ♡
